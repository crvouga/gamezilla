# Piece artwork licenses

## Cburnett

The chess pieces drawn by the board (the inline SVG `.piece` backgrounds in
[`pkgs/chess-board-root-client-ts/src/chess-board.module.css`](../../chess-board-root-client-ts/src/chess-board.module.css))
are the **Cburnett** set by Colin M.L. Burnett, taken from
`public/piece/cburnett/*.svg` in [lichess-org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila).

What each source states:

- [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces)
  publishes the originals under CC BY-SA 3.0 and the GFDL (see a file page such
  as `File:Chess_kdt45.svg`); this document previously also listed a BSD
  license.
- lichess-org/lila's `COPYING.md` lists `public/piece/cburnett` under GPLv2+.

Which of these terms this repository relies on, and the attribution the
shipped app owes, is pending legal review (see the repository's third-party
notices). Until then: keep the artwork's attribution to Cburnett here and in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](../../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), and do not modify
the SVGs.
