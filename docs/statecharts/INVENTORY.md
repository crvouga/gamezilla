The product has one authored machine with 436 states, 1,178 transitions and 46
guided journeys. All 39 registered public route patterns have state owners,
including alternative addresses for shared screens.
[STRUCTURE.md](STRUCTURE.md) shows the hierarchy, while
[EXECUTION_NOTES.md](EXECUTION_NOTES.md) records its automated traversal.
Framework examples retain their separate scope beneath the testing tool.

The testing tool's inspection region identifies sync diagnostics, client
configuration, live performance inspection, component references, individual
device details and the client-events placeholder. Their addresses and
existing adjacent contracts are visible, together with the missing observable
choices or outcomes. Additional undefined product behavior remains in the
authoritative inventory; the explorer does not fill those gaps by guessing.
